Questions, answered.

PriceIs Creator Space free?

Yes. Sign up with an email and everything on the card above is yours to use. There is no card to enter.

PlatformsWhich platforms can it read?

Any public YouTube channel, by its link. Live sign-in for Instagram is open, and TikTok, Facebook Pages and X follow platform by platform. Until yours is ready, type your numbers and you still see growth over time.

PrivacyWhere is my data kept?

In your own account, synced across your devices. Platform sign-ins are stored encrypted and can't be read from the browser. Other people see only what you share with them.

PhoneDoes it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your phone's browser, can be added to the home screen, opens without a connection, and sends the morning summary as a notification if you allow it.

TeamCan my editor or team use it?

Invite people by email to your whole workspace or to one space. Teammates get their own My work list, and when they finish something it moves to Review on your board.

AICan I use my own AI with it?

That is coming next. You will connect the AI you already pay for, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, with a one-time sign-in, and it will read your numbers and help plan your day.
