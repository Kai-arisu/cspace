Questions, answered.

Is Creator Space free?

Yes. Sign up with an email and everything on this page is yours to use. There is no card to enter.

Which platforms can it read?

Any public YouTube channel, by its link. Live sign-in for Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Pages and X is opening platform by platform. Until yours is ready, type your numbers and you still see growth over time.

Where is my data kept?

In your own account, synced across your devices. Platform sign-ins are stored encrypted and can't be read from the browser. Other people see only what you share with them.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in your phone's browser, can be added to the home screen, opens without a connection, and sends the morning summary as a notification if you allow it.

Can my editor or team use it?

Invite people by email to your whole workspace or to one space. Teammates get their own My work list, and when they finish something it moves to Review on your board.

Can I bring my stuff from other apps?

Drop a CSV or Excel file, or paste rows from Google Sheets, Trello or Notion, and match the columns to tasks, ideas, notes, posts, income, deals or audience numbers.
